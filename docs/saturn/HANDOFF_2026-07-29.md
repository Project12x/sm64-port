## Evidence chain for the coherency root cause (survives the crash)

- Pipe 5 ELF nm: `_s_transform_phase_ready` at `0x0026291A` (cached LWRAM);
  spans header absent (master stack local).
- LWRAM `0x00200000` **is cacheable**; only `0x2xxxxxxx` bypasses. The old
  comment claiming otherwise was the false premise.
- Stale-count mechanism explains both symptoms: cached `slave.count=0` at the
  join drops the slave terrain span (missing half wanders with the adaptive
  split); on stack-line eviction the true count floods the arena and Mario's
  reservation fails (`demo_emit_mario`). One header, two anti-correlated
  symptoms — why frustum (P4) and capacity (P5) patches failed.
- Upstream (pinned, inspected): SlaveDriver `WALLS.C:1806-1810` full cache
  flush per slave pass; `WALLS.C:1272,1356-1406` `cacheThruResult`
  cache-through writes. Our port kept the records discipline
  (`slavedriver_terrain_result.h`) but dropped it for fence/headers/bank.
